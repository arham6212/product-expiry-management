Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Tazaj ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our mobile application (the "App"), package name com.tazaj.expiry.

We are committed to protecting your privacy. We only collect the minimum amount of data necessary to provide and improve our expiry-date tracking and inventory management services.

1. Information We Collect and Process

Account and Authentication Data

To use the App's synchronized features, you must create an account. We collect your email address and a secure password. This authentication process is securely managed by our backend provider.

Shop, Inventory, and Product Information

The core purpose of the App is to manage store inventory. We collect and store the data you input, which includes:

  • Shop names, timezone, and currency preferences.
  • Product details, barcodes, selling prices, expiry dates, and batch information.
  • Inventory movements and shop membership roles (e.g., owner, manager, worker).

Camera and Barcode Scanning

The App requests access to your device's camera strictly for scanning product barcodes. Barcode scanning is processed securely on your device. We do not record or transmit video feeds from your camera.

Product Images and Photos

If you choose to upload product photos, the App will request access to your camera or photo gallery. Only the specific images you select are compressed and uploaded to our secure image hosting infrastructure to be associated with your inventory catalog. We do not access or collect any other photos from your device.

Device and Technical Information

To deliver over-the-air application updates and ensure app stability, we collect basic, non-personally identifiable technical data. This includes your operating system version, app version, and update patch number.

2. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We only share data with trusted service providers who act on our behalf to operate the App's infrastructure:

  • Supabase: Provides our backend database, secure authentication, and edge functions.
  • Cloudinary: Provides secure hosting, compression, and delivery for product images.
  • Shorebird: Delivers critical over-the-air application updates and bug fixes to your device.

3. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your account and inventory data for as long as your account is active. You have the right to request the deletion of your account and all associated data at any time.

How to Request Data Deletion

For detailed instructions on how to request the deletion of your account and personal data, please read our Account Deletion Policy.

4. Security

We take reasonable measures to help protect information about you from loss, theft, misuse, and unauthorized access. All network communications between the App and our backend services are encrypted using industry-standard protocols (HTTPS/TLS).

5. Children's Privacy

Our App is intended for business and retail management use. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If we become aware that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will take steps to delete such information.

6. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will notify you by updating the "Effective Date" at the top of this policy and, if necessary, providing a notice within the App.
