Tazaj Account Deletion

If you no longer wish to use Tazaj (com.tazaj.expiry), you have the right to request the permanent deletion of your account and personal data.

How to Request Account Deletion

To ensure the security of your account, we must verify your identity before processing a deletion request. Please follow these steps:

  1. Send an email to arhm6212@gmail.com.
  2. You must send the email from the exact email address registered to your Tazaj account.
  3. Use the subject line: Tazaj Account Deletion Request.
  4. In the body of the email, simply state that you wish to have your account permanently deleted.

What Data Will Be Deleted

Upon verification of your request, we will permanently delete the following personal data:

  • Your authentication credentials (email address and secure password).
  • Your user identifier and any personal profile information linked exclusively to your account.
  • Your shop membership records (your access to any specific shops will be permanently revoked).

What Data May Be Retained

Tazaj is a collaborative business inventory tool. To prevent data corruption and ensure that your shop's operations are not disrupted for other team members, the following business data will be retained after your personal account is deleted:

  • Inventory and Products: Any products, prices, barcodes, expiry dates, and inventory movements you created or modified will remain in the shop's database.
  • Images: Product photos you contributed to a shop or the global catalog will remain attached to those products.
  • Audit Records: Transaction logs and inventory movements associated with your past actions may be retained for security, auditing, and operational integrity for the shop owner. These records will be anonymized where possible, but the underlying business transaction data cannot be deleted.

Data Retention Period

Your account deletion request will be processed, and your personal authentication data will be deleted within 30 days of receiving your verified request. Retained business and audit data will be kept indefinitely as part of the shop's permanent operational records, unless the shop owner requests the complete deletion of their shop and all its associated data.
